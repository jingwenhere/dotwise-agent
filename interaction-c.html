From Dots to Direction

Last updated: August 2026 · Assigned to @DesignTeam · Under review

Ideas rarely arrive as finished arguments. They show up as fragments: a sentence, an image, a question. A useful note helps you capture the signal, connect it to context.

“A useful note does not preserve a thought. It gives the thought somewhere to go.”

1. Capture the dots

The best capture system is deliberately incomplete. Save the fragment before your brain turns it into a polished story; context can come later.

  • A sentence you do not want to lose
  • A question that keeps returning
  • An image that changes how you see the problem

2. Connect what keeps returning

A pattern of notes is revealing. Revisit the fragments that continue to feel unfinished and look for the questions, tensions, or images they share.
